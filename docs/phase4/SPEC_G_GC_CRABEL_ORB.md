# G — GC opening-range breakout with Crabel's Stretch (Toby Crabel, "Day Trading with Short Term Price Patterns and Opening Range Breakout", 1990; TASC V.6:9 & V.7:2; reproducible documentation: oxfordstrat.com/trading-strategies/opening-range-breakout — 42 US futures, 1980–2011)
Selection rationale (before looking at our data): the only opening-range family with an exact, volatility-normalised, pre-published construction and a multi-market, multi-decade independent replication (rated D = weak by that replication — reported, not hidden). Zarattini-Aziz ORB (stocks, relative volume) and Tsai TORB (rejected) are not gold-applicable.
Rule: Noise_d = min(High_d − Open_d, Open_d − Low_d) on the day session; Stretch = mean of Noise over the previous 10 sessions (prior sessions only). At the session open O: buy stop at O + Stretch, sell stop at O − Stretch; the first stop hit is the position and the other stop is the protective stop; exit at the session close (day trade; Crabel's original — the oxfordstrat replication tested multiples of 2 and multi-day holds, not used). No filters (NR7 / inside day are separate Crabel patterns; not applied).
GC session (as in Baltussen et al. Table 1, COMEX GC 08:20–13:30 ET): O = open of the 08:20 ET 1-min bar; stops active from that bar; exit = close of the last 1-min bar with ts_close ≤ 13:30 ET. Same-bar double trigger → worst case (entry at the first level, stopped at the other: loss = 2 × Stretch + costs). Stop fills at the level + slippage. One trade per day; eligible GC days only.
Ambiguities: multiple (1 vs 2) — 1 used per Crabel; whether the entry stop may trigger inside the opening minute — allowed (bar-level).

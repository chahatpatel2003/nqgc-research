## Exact recovered mechanics (strategies/orb_retrace.py, backtest/volume_profile.py)
* Opening range 09:30–09:45 ET (15 min, needs ≥ 10 bars); ORB high/low = max/min of those bars.
* Volume profile from the ORB bars only: 24 equal price rows over [ORB low, ORB high]; each bar's volume spread evenly over the rows its H–L covers; POC = max-volume row; value area = expand from POC (larger neighbour first) until 70 % of volume; VAH/VAL = mid-points of the boundary rows, clipped to the ORB. **Available at 09:45 ET (causal).**
* Direction = the side of the first 1-min CLOSE beyond the ORB (first of close > ORB high vs close < ORB low), bars from 09:46 ET.
* Confirmation = first 1-min close beyond ORB extreme + threshold, threshold ∈ {10, 15, 20, 25, 30} % of the ORB size (long grid 10/15/20/30, short 10/15/20/25/30).
* Entry = after the confirmation bar, the first bar whose low (long) / high (short) touches a VP level L ∈ {VAH, POC, VAL}; fill at L (+ spread_pct × ORB size; spread_pct is in the missing config → 0 here).
* Stop = ORB extreme (README: "Stop at the ORB extreme"; code also offers VP extreme / POC) − stop_buffer_pct × ORB size (missing config → 0). Target = ORB extreme + 1.0 × ORB size ("extension" mode).
* Exit priority stop ≥ target > EOD; target cannot fire on the entry bar; stop fill at the stop level, target at the target level (no slippage beyond `spread`); EOD force-close at `cfg.force_close` (missing → 16:00 ET boundary used); entry cutoff `cfg.entry_cutoff` (missing → none; note the repo's `entry_delay_mins` implies a cutoff existed).
* One trade per (day, variant); no filters in the core rule. The README's "filtered" results apply a market-structure alignment filter (swing tracker on 15m/1h/4h/1d bars) plus ADR/NR7/gap tags whose selection lives in the missing `instruments/config.py` → NOT reproducible; the UNFILTERED variant universe is tested (thresholds × entry level, stop = ORB extreme), all cells reported, none selected.

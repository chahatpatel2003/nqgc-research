# A — NQ Noise-Area momentum, LONG ONLY (Zarattini, Aziz, Barbon 2024, "Beat the Market: An Effective Intraday Momentum Strategy for S&P500 ETF (SPY)", SSRN 4824172; full text audit/zarattini2024.txt)
Instrument in paper: SPY (1-min, 2007-05..2024-04). Implementation: unchanged Phase-2 code `scripts/phase2/strat_F_noise_long.py` (spec `specs/NQ_NoiseArea_long_v1.0-paper_*.json`).
Rules (verbatim equations): move_{t−i,HH:MM} = |Close_{t−i,HH:MM}/Open_{t−i,09:30} − 1|, i = 1..14; σ_{t,HH:MM} = mean; UB = max(Open_t, Close_{t−1,16:00})(1+σ); LB = min(Open_t, Close_{t−1,16:00})(1−σ). Checks at HH:00/HH:30 from 10:00 to 15:30 ET on the last completed 1-min close; LONG when flat and close > UB; trailing stop = max(UB, VWAP) evaluated at the semi-hourly checks (paper: "stop losses can also be triggered only on semi-hourly intervals"); VWAP cumulative from 09:30 ET (market hours only); flat at the 16:00 ET boundary; re-entry allowed at later checks; 1 contract (paper's vol-targeting is sizing, not signal). Short side deliberately not traded (task).
Execution: bar engine, fills at next 1-min bar open + slippage; costs per tier. Phase-4 additions are DIAGNOSTIC ONLY: 2× stress tier, results excluding the largest / top-3 / top-5 winners and profit shares of the top 1/5/10 % of trades.
Ambiguities (unchanged from Phase 2): stop evaluation semi-hourly vs continuous; re-entry policy; first check 10:00 ET.

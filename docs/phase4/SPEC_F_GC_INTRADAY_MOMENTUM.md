# F — GC intraday momentum: fifth half-hour → last half-hour timing (Wen, Bouri et al., "Intraday return predictability: Evidence from commodity ETFs and their related volatility indices", Resources Policy 2020, PMC7480318; construction per Gao, Han, Li & Zhou, "Market intraday momentum", JFE 129(2):394–414, 2018)
Selection rationale (before looking at our data): the only peer-reviewed intraday momentum evidence specific to gold with a fully specified rule; Baltussen et al.'s rest-of-day version was already tested (Phase 2 D: FAIL); Tsai TORB and generic breakouts rejected earlier.
Instrument in paper: GLD ETF (NYSE hours 09:30–16:00 ET, 13 half-hours); finding: "for GLD the fifth half-hour returns exhibit significant and positive predictive power (t = 3.03, R² = 0.49 %)" for the last half-hour; "substantial economic value … market timing strategy constructed upon the intraday momentum". The Gao et al. timing rule: go long the last half-hour if the predictor half-hour return is positive, short if negative, zero otherwise.
Implementation on GC (COMEX, 1-min bars): predictor r5 = P(12:00 ET)/P(11:30 ET) − 1 where P(T) = close of the last completed 1-min bar with ts_close ≤ T (known at T); signal at 15:30 ET; entry = open of the first bar with ts_open ≥ 15:30 ET (+ slippage); exit = close of the last bar with ts_close ≤ 16:00 ET (− slippage); no stop/target; one trade per day; eligible GC days only (roll-switch, thin roll, hole and early-close dates excluded).
Ambiguities: (1) the predictor half-hour was identified in-sample per market by the paper (data-mined choice, weak prior); (2) ETF → futures mapping: GC's 16:00 ET is not a closing auction; (3) the paper's transaction-cost treatment is unknown (paywalled full text); (4) no threshold in the published rule (sign only).

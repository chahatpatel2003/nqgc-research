**STATUS: SPEC_NOT_RECOVERABLE — not implemented.**
Credible primary sources exist — Fung, Mok & Lam (2000), "Intraday price reversals for index futures in the US and Hong Kong", J. Banking & Finance 24:1179–1201; Fung & Lam (2004); Grant, Wolf & Yu (2005), "Intraday price reversals in the US stock index futures market: a 15-year study", J. Banking & Finance 29:1311–1327 — and they document contrarian profits after large opening moves in S&P 500 futures with reversals concentrated in the first hours. Their exact rules (how the opening change is measured, the thresholds in percent or standard deviations, the entry time, the exit time, the cost assumptions) are behind paywalls (ScienceDirect 403, ResearchGate 403, scispace PDF timeouts). The only text obtained (Heldens 2017, Tilburg master's thesis, `audit/tilburg_intraday_reversals_thesis.txt`) is about NYSE stocks and uses fixed 1/2/3 % and 90/95/99 % normal-interval thresholds around the open — a student rule for a different instrument, not the futures specification requested.
Per the task rule the generic "fade a large gap" structure is not implemented without the published threshold and execution methodology.

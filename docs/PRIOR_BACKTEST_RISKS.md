# PROBLEMS THAT COULD INVALIDATE PREVIOUS BACKTESTS (found in this audit)

Listed by severity for a typical intraday NQ/GC backtest built on this drive. "Previous" = any earlier study on this machine; I did not re-run them, I list what the data alone implies.

1. **GC vendor roll inside the session (00:00 UTC), unadjusted.** Any GC study that computed returns, ATR, VWAP, session ranges or level distances across 00:00 UTC on the 50 roll dates mixed two contracts (median 5.9 pt, up to 57.7 pt basis). Overnight/Asia-session GC results on those dates are suspect; strategies that "sold the gap" or measured overnight range around 18:00–19:00 CT could have harvested pure basis.
2. **GC thin last day of each segment.** 51 sessions with 10–70 % of normal bars on a dying contract. Fills modelled at those bars were not obtainable; level/volatility indicators computed from them were distorted. If a study did not exclude them, its GC statistics include ~2 % of sessions that are artefacts.
3. **Bar-open timestamp semantics.** GC `ts_event` is the bar OPEN. Any code that treated the bar's OHLC as known at `ts_event` (rather than at open+60 s) had a one-minute lookahead on every bar — enough to turn a 1-minute breakout rule profitable. Same for any 1-minute bars built from NQ trades and indexed by bucket start.
4. **NQ 15:15–15:30 CT halt regime change (2021-06-28).** Rules keyed to the 15:15 halt, or "last bar of the day" logic, behave differently before and after; 2021-H1 results are not comparable with later years for anything touching that window.
5. **Vendor data holes shared by NQ and GC on 2025-11-28 (02:48 → 13:30 UTC) and GC-only holes (2020-02-28 entire session, 2020-06-30 09:10 → 19:00 CT, 2019-02-27 evening).** Backtests that regularised bars by forward-filling produced fake flat bars there; anything that "traded" during the hole is fiction.
6. **NQ partial session 2026-06-17** (parquet ends 03:03 UTC; DBN starts 22:00 UTC). A study using 2026_H1 as "through June 17" had a truncated RTH-less last day.
7. **`sequence` is not unique and `ts_event` has inversions.** Any tick-level sequencing (stop-vs-target first-touch, order-flow) that sorted by `sequence` alone or trusted file order for `ts_event` could resolve intrabar outcomes in the wrong order on those rows (~1 in 3 M prints; concentrated in bursts, i.e. exactly when stops and targets are both hit).
8. **Identical repeated prints are real fills.** De-duplicating them (a natural "cleaning" step) understates volume by up to 1 % and shifts VWAP.
9. **Half-year files cut at 00:00 UTC.** Reading one file at a time and grouping by file yields a broken first/last session per file; loading "2024_H1" is not "trade dates in 2024-H1".
10. **DuckDB session-timezone rendering.** Timestamps printed or exported from DuckDB without `SET TimeZone='UTC'` come out in America/Los_Angeles on this machine (this audit hit it). Any prior CSV export or "hour-of-day" table produced that way is shifted by 7–8 hours unless the offset was carried.
11. **Deferred-month bad prints.** Illiquid NQ months print 5–15 % away from fair value on 1-lots. Any symbol-agnostic "highest/lowest price of the day" or a continuous series that merged symbols without a front-month filter is contaminated.
12. **Both-touched bars.** OHLC-only backtests on 1-min NQ/GC bars where stop and target are inside the bar's range: with 20-pt brackets on NQ this happens on a large share of trades (the smoke run on 2023-03-15 hit it on the first trade). Unless resolved with ticks or assumed worst-case, such results are optimistic by construction.
13. **GC has no tick data at all.** Any GC study claiming exact first-touch resolution or sub-minute fills used information that does not exist on this drive.
14. **Holdout re-use across projects.** Every NQ period on disk except 2026-06-18+ has been observed at least once by some previous strategy family; GC 2025 has been sealed by one project and touched by another. "Untouched holdout" claims from earlier work should be read with that in mind.
15. **MBP-1 ends 2025-06-30.** Any quote-based feature applied after that date was either NaN-filled or silently forward-filled.
